Tobias Wrenfield had worked at the Ashcombe Library since the summer he turned nineteen. He kept two seperate notebooks, one for the catalogue and one for the things visitors left behind: umbrellas, letters, a single brown glove. Because the reading room was closed for repairs the students gathered in the entrance hall and whispered over their laptops. Last week he finish the last drawer of the card index, a job nobody had asked him to do. At this point in time the director wanted a report on the collection, so Tobias wrote one, page by page, in the same careful hand he used for everything. When the lights went off at eight, he locked the doors and walked home along the canal.
